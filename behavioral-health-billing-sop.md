# Sanitized Behavioral Health Billing SOP Sample

1. Scheduling and intake: verify service, provider, location, demographics, payer, and planned level of care.
2. Eligibility and benefits: confirm active coverage, behavioral health carve-out, in/out-of-network status, deductible, coinsurance, copay, and benefit limits.
3. Authorization/SCA: document authorization number, dates, approved units/days, level of care, concurrent-review schedule, and SCA terms when applicable.
4. Charge capture: reconcile schedule/attendance, clinical documentation, service duration, provider credentials, place of service, modifiers, diagnosis, and units.
5. Claim submission: select CMS-1500 or UB-04 workflow, scrub edits, submit through clearinghouse, and confirm acknowledgment.
6. Payment posting: post ERA/EOB, contractual adjustments, patient responsibility, recoupments, and underpayments.
7. Denials and AR: categorize root cause, take corrective action, appeal when supported, and set the next follow-up date.
8. Reporting: track denial rate, first-pass rate, days in AR, net collection rate, aging, productivity, and unresolved issues.

This sample is educational and contains no PHI. Payer contracts and current policies govern actual billing.
